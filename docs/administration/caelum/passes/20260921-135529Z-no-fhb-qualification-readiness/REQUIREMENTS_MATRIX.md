## Retry and evidence budgets

- Natural proof: initial planned validation plus at most two additional attempts
  across the pass, each following a named correction or demonstrated operator/
  setup fault. An unchanged rerun is forbidden.
- Formal capacity: fixed contract repeats; after a failed series, at most one
  corrected rerun following a named repair.
- Failed epochs, contaminated timing, and negative outcomes remain preserved.
  A green suffix cannot replace an admitted failure interval.
